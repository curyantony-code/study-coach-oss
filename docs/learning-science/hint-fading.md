# Fading hints

## The principle

Worked examples and other support help novices and can hinder students who already know the material (the expertise-reversal effect).

## What Study Coach does

- Each note has a mastery level. Below 40% its cards come with a cue; between 40% and 70% the cue is offered on request; above 70% there is none.
- For a worked problem the cue is the required formula. For other cards it is how the answer starts and how long it is, and only when the answer is long enough that this does not give it away.
- Whether a hint was seen is recorded with the answer.
- Not yet done: the handover asks for stronger fading in `kind: procedural` courses; the thresholds are the same for every course.
