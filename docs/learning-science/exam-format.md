# Practise in the exam’s format

## The principle

Practice transfers best when it resembles the test: the same kind of question, under similar conditions.

## What Study Coach does

- Each assessment in `study.yml` lists its format; a mock exam for it uses multiple choice, written answers, or both accordingly.
- Mock exams are timed and give no feedback until the paper is submitted.
- In the two weeks before an assessment its cards are reviewed more often, and each is brought back at least once in the final three days.

## Where in the code

- `lib/learner/exam.ts`
- `lib/learner/schedule.ts` (`clampDueForExam`)
