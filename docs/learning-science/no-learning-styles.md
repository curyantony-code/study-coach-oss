# No learning styles

## The principle

Matching teaching to a student's supposed learning style (visual, auditory, …) has not been shown to improve learning. Active work — generating, enacting, drawing — helps students in general.

## What Study Coach does

- The app never labels a student by style. Intake asks about habits and what they enjoy.
- Those answers only change which modes are suggested (for example Match and Arcade for someone who likes games).
- Write, Match and Arcade stay because producing and manipulating answers is active work.
