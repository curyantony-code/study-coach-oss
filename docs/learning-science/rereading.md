# Rereading is not study

## The principle

The same 2013 review rates rereading and highlighting as low-utility: they feel productive and barely help.

## What Study Coach does

- Reading a note in the Library records nothing and changes no schedule.
- Worked solutions in a note are folded away, so reading the note does not hand over the answer before an attempt.
- Asking the AI about a highlight, or watching a suggested video, is also reference: neither counts as an attempt.
- The intake plan flags rereading, highlighting and summarising when a student lists them as their methods.
